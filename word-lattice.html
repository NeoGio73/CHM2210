Orgo Word Lattice

Key terms from OpenStax Organic Chemistry, hidden in a cube of letters. Every line through the cube can hide a word, including lines that pass through the middle.

Find these terms

    How to play
    1. Drag to turn the cube. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
    2. Tap the first letter of a term. Only letters in a straight line from it stay lit.
    3. Tap the last letter. If the letters between spell a term, they bond in color.
    4. Words can run through the inside of the cube. Use Spread to open the gaps and Peel to cut away the front layers.
    5. Spaces, hyphens, apostrophes and accents are left out in the cube.
